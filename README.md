Entry for the make.fun competition [One Line of Earned Pride](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557).

## The entry

> The night janitor who studied between shifts for six years opened the exam envelope, read 'pass' twice, and quietly mopped the last corridor like it was his.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief (genuine earned pride first, a concrete scene with a real person, pride earned inside the line and never asserted, lands by the last word, freshness away from trophy/podium/graduation). This one won: six years of studying between shifts is felt effort, the envelope read twice is a seen moment, and the pride arrives quietly in the final image of the last corridor mopped 'like it was his'. The alternatives (a password typed correctly at 61 and the good biscuit; a chord practiced three winters in a garage) were charming but thinner in earned weight or more familiar in shape.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed `LINE` constant to `entry.txt`, validates length/single-line/non-blank before writing, and reads the file back byte for byte. Running it twice produces identical bytes (sha256 598ca38546832fd8f1a9b365d5163a42aa35e147696957d894c156e348cc0111).
